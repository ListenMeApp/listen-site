How it works

About a minute from download to your first map.

  1. Connect Apple Music. Sign in and allow access to your listening history.
  2. Watch it light up. Listen reads what you play and finds where every artist comes from.
  3. Keep listening. Your map, charts and stats update in the background on their own.

Questions

Do I need an Apple Music subscription?

Yes — Listen builds your map and stats from your Apple Music listening history, so an active Apple Music subscription is needed to connect it. Without one you can still sign in with your Apple ID, look around and play 30-second previews.

Does it work with Spotify?

Not yet. Listen is built for Apple Music on iPhone. Tell us at hi@listenapp.site if you want it for another service — requests decide what comes next.

How much does it cost?

Listen Premium starts with a 3-day free trial, then renews annually or monthly — prices are shown in the app in your local currency. Cancel any time in your App Store settings.

What happens to my data?

We use your listening history only to build your map and stats. No ads, nothing sold, nothing posted unless you tap share. Deleting your account in the app erases all of it. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Why isn't my old listening on the map yet?

Apple shares only your recent history, so your map starts with what you've played lately and grows as you keep listening — older favourites appear as soon as you play them again.
